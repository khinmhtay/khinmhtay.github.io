Hi, I am Khin Maung Htay, a Trust & Safety data analyst at Accenture Malaysia. I previously earned my Research Master's degree in Computer Enginnering from Universiti Malaysia Perlis (UniMAP), where I was grateful to be served as a Graduate Research Assistant for a Fundamental Research Grant Scheme (FRGS) project funded by the Ministry of Education Malaysia. My research interests center on combinatorial optimization, evolutionary and metaheuristic algorithms, and aritifical intellgience applications in healthcare. Prior to my graduate studies, I gained hands-on research experience through internships at the Electronic Systems Lab at the International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM)—under the advisement of Prof. Dr. Anis Nurashikin Nordin—and at 3D Synapsis.

In my personal time, I explore visual storytelling through street photography, play badminton, and go hiking.
